Our story

How STOMP started

STOMP began with one queer line-dancing night in Amsterdam-Noord and a simple idea: everyone deserves a dance floor where they belong. What started as a handful of friends learning a few steps has grown into a recurring night where strangers become a line, and a line becomes a crowd.

We blend the fun of a proper night out with the structure of real line-dance training — beginner-friendly by design, queer and welcoming by default. No partner required, no experience needed, and absolutely no judgment. Just boots (optional), big country tunes, and a room full of people cheering for whoever's learning.

Come for the dances, stay for the people. yee + haw.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?

Never. Line dancing is danced in lines — you'll dance with the whole room.

Do I need experience or boots?

Nope. We teach everything from scratch. Wear comfy shoes; boots are welcome but never required.

Is it really for everyone?

Yes. STOMP is an explicitly queer, all-are-welcome space.
